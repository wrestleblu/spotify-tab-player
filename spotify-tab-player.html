Connect your Spotify

Your Client ID is saved in this page. Log in with your Spotify Premium account and this page becomes a Spotify Connect device named “Tab Player” — press play here, or pick “Tab Player” in Spotify on your phone/computer.

Tap / long-press the address to select and copy it if you need it for the Spotify dashboard.

If login fails with redirect_uri_mismatch, the URI in your Spotify app (developer.spotify.com/dashboard → your app → Settings) differs from the one above by even one character — update it to match exactly, save, and try again. APIs enabled on the app: Web API and Web Playback SDK.

Scopes requested at login: streaming, user-read-playback-state, user-modify-playback-state, user-read-email, user-read-private, playlist-modify-private (the last one is what lets “Save to Spotify” create a private playlist in your account). Login uses Spotify's PKCE flow — no Client Secret is needed or stored. Your access token is kept in this tab's session only and is gone when the tab closes.

Already connected before playlist saving was added? Your existing login doesn't have the new playlist-modify-private permission yet — use Disconnect below, then Log in with Spotify again, and Spotify will grant it. Browsing and playback work either way; only saving a playlist needs the fresh login.
