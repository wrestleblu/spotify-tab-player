Connect your Spotify

Your Client ID is saved in this page. Log in with your Spotify Premium account and this page becomes a Spotify Connect device named “Tab Player” — press play here, or pick “Tab Player” in Spotify on your phone/computer.

Redirect URI registered in your Spotify app — Spotify requires it to be the exact address where this player page is hosted (for your deployment, the GitHub Pages address you registered in the Spotify dashboard). The login flow sends that same hosted address automatically; there is nothing to copy here.

If login fails with redirect_uri_mismatch, the URI in your Spotify app (developer.spotify.com/dashboard → your app → Settings) differs from this page's hosted address by even one character — update it to match exactly, save, and try again. APIs enabled on the app: Web API and Web Playback SDK.

Scopes requested at login: streaming, user-read-playback-state, user-modify-playback-state, user-read-email, user-read-private, playlist-modify-private (what lets “Save to Spotify” create a private playlist in your account), playlist-read-private and playlist-read-collaborative (what let “Your playlists” list and open your own playlists, private ones included). Login uses Spotify's PKCE flow — no Client Secret is needed or stored. Your login is saved on this device only (localStorage — the same place your My Tabs token lives), so when you come back you're reconnected automatically: the access token quietly refreshes itself before it expires, no login tap needed. Use Disconnect to clear the login from this device.

Already connected before playlist saving was added? Your existing login doesn't have the new playlist-modify-private permission yet — use Disconnect below, then Log in with Spotify again, and Spotify will grant it. Browsing and playback work either way; only saving a playlist needs the fresh login.
